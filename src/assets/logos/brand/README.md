# The coins' official marks

Each tool's own logo, in its own colours, as its maker publishes it. Both coins
(the hero's dot on the i and the stage's coin) draw their faces from these,
through `src/webgl/coinMarks.js`.

Black-and-white brands come in two versions: `-on-dark` for a dark coin (the day
scheme's ink) and `-on-light` for a light one (the night scheme). The rest work
on either.

Retrieved 2026-09-25:

- svgl.app (official brand SVGs): threejs-light/-dark, blender, react_light/_dark,
  figma, framer/framer_dark, webflow, vite, nextjs_icon_dark, shopify,
  lottiefiles, javascript — https://svgl.app/library/<name>.svg
- GSAP: gsap-black.svg / gsap-white.svg from GSAP's brand assets,
  https://gsap.com/brand (gsap-brand-assets.zip)
- Spline: spline_logo png from https://spline.design (their site's own logo)
- svgl.app, retrieved 2026-10-04: angular (for the About's ruler, the years before
  the 3D) — https://svgl.app/library/angular.svg
- custom-code.svg: drawn for this site, not a brand's: `</>` on JavaScript's
  yellow, for sites written by hand (React, Next.js, GSAP, Three.js and the rest)

The coins show only the tools the hero names, in its order (`COIN_SKILLS` in
coinMarks.js); the other marks stay here for later use.

The logos are the trademarks of their owners, used here to name the tools the
work was made with.
